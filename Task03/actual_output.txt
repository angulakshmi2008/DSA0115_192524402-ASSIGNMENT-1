--- System Data Type Sizes (bytes) ---
sizeof(bool)        : 1 byte
sizeof(char)        : 1 byte
sizeof(int)         : 4 bytes
sizeof(unsigned int): 4 bytes
sizeof(long long)   : 8 bytes
sizeof(float)       : 4 bytes
sizeof(double)      : 8 bytes

--- Integer Limits & Network Capacity ---
INT_MAX             : 2147483647 (2147483647)

--- IEEE-754 Floating-Point Precision Flaws ---
0.1 + 0.2 = 0.30000000000000004
Does 0.1 + 0.2 == 0.3? false (FALSE!)
10.80 stored as double: 10.800000000000001
10 times 0.10 == 1.00? true
=== Measurement of Floating-Point Drift Across n Additions ===
Testing fare: Rs 10.80 (1080 paise - e.g. discounted rate with surcharge)

  n Journeys | Exact Total (Rs) | Double Total (Rs) | Absolute Drift (Rs)
-------------+------------------+------------------+--------------------
          10 |           108.00 |           108.00 |      -1.421085e-14
        1000 |         10800.00 |         10800.00 |      -7.821654e-11
      100000 |       1080000.00 |       1080000.00 |       1.996988e-06
     1000000 |      10800000.00 |      10800000.00 |       6.423146e-05
     3000000 |      32400000.00 |      32400000.00 |       1.554348e-03
    10000000 |     108000000.00 |     107999999.98 |      -1.890907e-02

Conclusion: In integer paise, the error is always identically ZERO.
In double rupees, rounding error accumulates continuously with n.
